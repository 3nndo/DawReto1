Who we are


EcoShark is developing a platform that connects sustainable event organizers with volunteers to arrange clean-ups and waste collection activities.


Our team has shared its CVs here for everyone to view.

Environmental commitment

Volunteer management

Our platform will allow organizers to publish clean-up activities and manage volunteer registrations. This will make it easier to coordinate participants, organize teams, and distribute tasks according to each event's needs, encouraging more efficient collaboration.

Technological innovation

We develop web solutions to improve the organization of sustainable events. Our application is designed for both events at a fixed location, such as concerts and festivals, and sporting competitions held along a route, such as marathons and cycling races. This allows us to adapt volunteer management to the specific needs of each activity.

Our project

We are developing a web application to help event organizers manage volunteers and coordinate clean-up tasks. In this way, we aim to make collaboration easier and reduce the environmental impact of every event.
